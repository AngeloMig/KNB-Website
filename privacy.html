Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and your choices — in plain language. This policy covers this website and our contact form.

Last updated · 29 June 2026

Information we collect

When you contact us or submit our enquiry form, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address

  • Your website URL
  • Project details and any message you send us

Our hosting provider may also collect standard technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, as part of normal server operation and security.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry

We only keep your information for as long as needed for these purposes.

Third-party services

Our website relies on third-party services to operate, including form handling and hosting (Netlify), and spam protection (Google reCAPTCHA). We may also use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the site is used.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information when it is necessary to provide our services, operate the website, or comply with a legal obligation.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, update, or delete the personal information you have shared with us. To do so, email us using the address below and we’ll handle your request promptly.
